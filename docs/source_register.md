# Source Register

Provenance for every data source. "Limitations" is the most important column in
this project — it is where the ethical spine lives.

| # | Source | URL | License / usage | Date accessed | Granularity | Refresh | Known issues | Fields used (rough) | Transformations | Limitations & bias risks |

| 1 | HMDA Modified LAR (FFIEC/CFPB) | https://ffiec.cfpb.gov/data-browser/ · schema: https://ffiec.cfpb.gov/documentation/publications/modified-lar/modified-lar-schema | Public U.S. government data (HMDA / Reg C); free, attribute CFPB/FFIEC | 2026-08-04 | One row per loan application/decision per year; down to census tract | Annual | "Information not provided" demographic codes are not null; rate_spread populated only for some loans; small-filer partial exemptions | action_taken, derived_race, derived_ethnicity, derived_sex, loan_amount, interest_rate, rate_spread, loan_type, loan_purpose, occupancy_type, census_tract, county_code, state_code, lei, debt_to_income_ratio | Decode codes; restrict to decisioned actions (later tasks) | Public Modified LAR redacts credit score entirely and reports DTI/age as ranges; lenders had this data at decision time but it is withheld from the public file, so disparities cannot be attributed to creditworthiness and are signals for review only |
| 2 | U.S. Census ACS 5-Year Estimates (tract) | https://data.census.gov · API: https://api.census.gov/data | Public U.S. government data; free with API key | 2026-08-04 | Census tract | Annual (5-year rolling) | Estimates carry margins of error, larger on small tracts; tract boundaries shifted with 2020 redistricting | Median household income (B19013), race/ethnicity (B03002), tenure/owner-occupancy (B25003) | Join to HMDA on 11-digit tract; align ACS vintage to HMDA year | 5-year values are period averages, not point-in-time; sampling error; must match ACS vintage to each HMDA year or geography drifts |
| 3 | FFIEC HMDA Filers / Institution panel | https://ffiec.cfpb.gov/v2/reporting/filers/2023 (swap year) · https://ffiec.cfpb.gov/v2/public/institutions | Public U.S. government data; free | 2026-08-04 | One row per filing institution per year | Annual | Institutions merge/rename across years; LEI is the stable key; panel covers filers only | LEI, institution name, agency code, asset size/type where available | Join to HMDA on LEI to build dim_lender | Reflects only institutions that filed; small-filer partial exemptions leave some fields blank |
| 4 | FRED — 30-yr mortgage rate & FHFA HPI | https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/MORTGAGE30US · https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/USSTHPI | Public via FRED; MORTGAGE30US originates from Freddie Mac and is copyrighted — review series notes before redistributing | 2026-08-04 | Time series (weekly rate; quarterly HPI) | Weekly / quarterly | Frequency mismatch with annual HMDA; national vs MSA granularity differs | 30-yr fixed mortgage rate (MORTGAGE30US), FHFA House Price Index (USSTHPI) | Aggregate to annual; use to normalize rate_spread and add market context | Context/benchmark only — not loan-level; national series won't capture local variation |
